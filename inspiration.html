💡 Project Idea Starters

Quick prompts to get your brain going — pick one and make it your own

💧 Clean Water

  • Can a homemade filter clean dirty water?
  • Which natural material absorbs the most water?
  • How does temperature affect how fast water evaporates?
  • Do plants grow better with rainwater or tap water?

⚡ Energy

  • Which color absorbs the most solar heat?
  • Can you build a solar oven that melts chocolate?
  • What insulates a cup of hot water best?
  • Does wind direction affect how a pinwheel spins?

🌱 Food & Agriculture

  • Do plants grow faster with music?
  • Which fruit produces the most electricity?
  • Can you grow a plant from kitchen scraps?
  • What soil grows the tallest bean plant?

♻️ Sustainability

  • Which material decomposes fastest in soil?
  • How much trash does your family make in a week?
  • Can you make your own paper from old paper?
  • What recycled materials make the strongest bridge?

🩺 Health & Body

  • Does hand sanitizer work better than soap?
  • How does sleep affect memory?
  • Which apple stays fresh longest in different liquids?
  • Does music change your heart rate?

🚀 Space & Weather

  • Can you predict tomorrow's weather by looking at clouds?
  • How does wind affect the temperature outside?
  • Build a rain gauge — how much rain fell this week?
  • Why does the moon look bigger near the horizon?

🦋 Animals & Nature

  • Do ants prefer sugar or protein?
  • What's living in a drop of pond water?
  • How do birds find food in winter?
  • Which bird seed attracts the most birds?

🛠️ Engineering & Building

  • What bridge design holds the most weight?
  • Can you build a tower using only paper and tape?
  • What makes a paper airplane fly farthest?
  • Which boat design holds the most pennies before sinking?

📚 All resources are free and from official sources. Capri Elementary PTA does not endorse any specific program — these are starting points for your own exploration.
